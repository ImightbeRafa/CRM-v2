The short version

Prices and safety answers are right almost every time. The bots lose sales in three places. They stop after the first price answer with a soft yes/no question. They say "Dame un momento" for things they already know. And the follow-ups use the same generic line for everyone. One real code bug made all of this worse: on Instagram, about 1 in 20 bot messages was saved as if a person wrote it. When that happened the rest of the reply was dropped and later customer messages were ignored.

The order count only includes orders linked to the chat. Orders the team typed in by hand without a link are missing, so the real rate is somewhat higher.

What goes wrong

1

Bot messages saved as a person, rest of the reply dropped

code bugfixed in PR #160

Instagram sends back a copy ("echo") of every message the bot sends. When that copy was saved first, the bot's own save was thrown away as a duplicate. The message then looked like a person wrote it. The bot's next bubble checked "did a person reply?", saw it, and stopped. Often the lost bubble was the price or the question.

Sleeping · Mauren · 10-27 00:39"De Pérez Zeledón, cuánto cuestan ?" → only "¡Hola! Soy Vale de Sleeping Patches CR 🌙" went out. No price, no follow-up.

7 days: 129 replies and 85 follow-ups saved as a person; 47 replies cut short; 140 customer messages skipped in chats no person had taken over.

Fix: when the bot's save matches the echo of the same message, the bot's mark now wins. A person's own messages are never relabeled. Committed on top of PR #160, tests added.

2

The chat stops at the first price answer

sales flowall stores

About 3 in 4 chats are one ad question ("¿Cuánto cuesta…?"), one bot answer ending in a soft yes/no question, then silence. The bot rarely gives a reason to buy, rarely offers the pack saving, and asks something the customer has to think about.

Forge · typical"El arnés cuesta ₡14 900… ¿Querés que te pase la guía de tallas?" → no reply. When people did say "Sí", the bot often asked the same question again instead of sending the guide.
PatchHouse · typical"₡9 900. ¿Cuál parche te interesa: Focus, Energy, Stress Relief o Dopamine?" → no reply. No recommendation, no combo.

Fix: the first answer gives the price, one line of value, and one easy next step. Forge: send the size guide right away and ask height and weight. Sleeping: "2 bolsas ₡19 800, te ahorrás el envío". PatchHouse: ask what they want to improve, then recommend one. When the customer says "Sí", do the thing that was offered. Never ask the same question twice.

3

"Dame un momento" for things the bot already knows

hand-off rulesknowledge gaps

A hand-off makes the customer wait 15 min to 19 h, and the bot stays silent the whole time. Most were avoidable:

  • Model errors (24): on 10-22 the AI provider failed for two hours, and every price question got "Dame un momento y te confirmo".
  • Blocked drafts (~27): the safety check threw away a normal answer to "¿Cuánto cuesta un paquete?", "¿Hay descuento por mayor?" or "¿Son seguros con alergias?" and sent the waiting line instead.
  • Forge sizes out of the table (~15): "Mido 1.80 pero peso 65 kg" → "no quiero recomendarte una talla equivocada". Staff answered hours later with the same rule every time: weight decides.
  • Missing facts: Forge's WhatsApp number, how to wash the harness, delivery to a post office, pickup hours, bulk discount, and the GLP-1 waitlist that staff already run.
  • Night line on easy questions: "A esta hora te atiende nuestro asistente virtual… el equipo te escribe a las 9:00". That goes against "night sells like day".

Fix: on a model error, retry once, then send a reply built from the store's own data (price, pack, shipping) instead of the waiting line. Give a blocked draft one rewrite before handing off. Add the missing facts to each store's knowledge, including Forge's weight-first size rule and the waitlist.

4

Follow-ups: one generic line, once, at any stage

follow-ups

There are six texts in total, two per store. Each chat gets one ever, about 2 to 20 hours later, whatever happened in the chat.

Wrong moments seen: after the customer already gave size and address or got the SINPE details; after "espero la quincena" or "ahorita no tengo plata"; "¿Ya conocés cómo funciona?" to people who already got the explanation. Missing ones: SINPE details sent but no receipt, with no reminder.

Fix: write the follow-up from where the chat stopped (the product, size or question they left open), for example "¿Te reservo el XL? Solo ocupo tu dirección". Skip anyone who already gave data or paid. Respect a date they named. Add a payment reminder after SINPE details. Allow a second touch at about 24 h, still inside Meta's window. The direct question about one product has the best reply rate, so lean that way.

5

The data form scares buyers off

sales flow

About 8 of 10 customers who reached "pasame estos datos" with the 7-field list never answered. Pura Sonrisa sent the form and the payment menu together before the flavor was even picked.

Fix: one question per bubble, in order: product/size → home or pickup → name and address in one free line → payment method. The chats that converted with no person all followed that order and took under 15 min.

6

False payment hand-offs from an older message

PR #160 covers part

PR #160 fixes the cases from your screenshots ("Qué costó tiene…", "De cuando sería el monto?", "Muchas gracias" after a receipt). The audit still shows hand-offs triggered by an earlier payment message ("Muy buenas tardes", "¿Solo un modelo tiene?", "No tienen un manual?" after delivery, "Tarjeta contra entrega :)" as a plain choice). 26 hand-offs in 7 days were flagged as likely false.

Fix: after merge, rerun handoff-audit.ts and see which are left. A hand-off should come from the newest message, not a SINPE word from earlier in the chat.

Keep

What already works

  • Prices are correct in practically every chat (₡14 900 / ₡26 900; ₡9 900 + ₡3 000 / ₡19 800 / ₡28 500).
  • Health and safety answers are careful: no claims, "mayores de 18", "consultá a tu médico".
  • Payment method is asked before the SINPE details, and receipts go to a person quickly.
  • When a customer cooperates, the bot closes alone in 4 to 15 minutes (Felipe, Samuel, Alex on Forge; Yese on Sleeping; Lau on PatchHouse).
For Rafael and the team

Decisions and data to fix by hand

  • Prices staff type differ from the bot: Sleeping "2 por ₡21 900" and "2 por ₡22 800" vs the bot's ₡19 800. PatchHouse "2X ₡20 900" vs the bot's combo ₡22 800. The site is the source of truth; staff saved replies need updating.
  • Pickup hours and shipping time differ: bot "todos los días 9 a 6" vs staff "L–V 9–6:30, S 9–3". Bot 2–3 días vs staff 1–2 días.
  • "WASCR-QUARKCR" appears to customers as a pickup place name.
  • Risk: a staff reply told an upset PatchHouse customer the patches are "aprobados por la FDA".
  • Pura Sonrisa: after staff set up contra entrega, the bot answered "Muchas gracias" with the SINPE details.
  • Still open from PR #160: "Ya pagué" with no photo. Ask for the comprobante, or hand off right away?
  • New question: after a hand-off, may the bot keep answering non-payment questions (delivery time, sizes) if no person replied within, say, 10 minutes?
